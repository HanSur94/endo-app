    - For window N (last7=7, last30=30, last90=90), the window is `[today-(N-1) days … today]` inclusive on start-of-day; a snapshot dated within it counts, one outside does not. A day exactly N-1 back is IN; N back is OUT.

    Per-symptom severity mean + frequency (D-05/D-07):
    - Mean severity for a SymptomType = mean of non-nil severities over days the symptom is logged; a day where the symptom is logged WITHOUT a severity (severity nil) is excluded from the mean denominator but may count toward frequency.
    - Frequency = days-present / days-with-any-log in the window.
    Honesty — absence ≠ 0 (D-03/Pitfall 2):
    - A day with NO stress reading does NOT change the stress mean (excluded from denominator); a logged stress of 0 DOES (included as 0). A fixture asserts the two produce DIFFERENT means.
    Direction (D-07):
    - direction compares the recent window `[today-(N-1)…today]` against the immediately-prior equal-length window `[today-(2N-1)…today-N]`; up/down/steady, with steady when the delta is within a small band. allTime has no prior window → no direction.
    Min-data threshold (D-08):
    - Below the named per-trend threshold of logged days in the window, the engine emits an explicit "not enough data" .trend Insight OR omits that trend — never a misleading one. A fixture with sub-threshold data asserts this.
