Make three release declarations mechanically true and drift-proof against the codebase: (1) encode the Data-safety form answers as a committed machine-checkable `DataSafetyDeclaration` constant and cross-check its all-"No" answers against the manifest (no INTERNET), the version catalog (no analytics/backend), and the read-only health posture (no health WRITE); (2) assert `targetSdk`/`compileSdk` == 36 (with `minSdk` 26) and that the release build is not debuggable; (3) add ONE `PRIVACY_POLICY_URL` single-source constant and assert it is the only privacy-policy URL literal, non-blank and well-formed.

Output: `DataSafetyDeclaration` + three red-capable guard tests, all GREEN, plus a single committed `PRIVACY_POLICY_URL` placeholder the user finalizes before submission.
</objective>
