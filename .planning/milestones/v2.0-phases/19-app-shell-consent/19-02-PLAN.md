<objective>
Stand up the single-activity app entry and the consent-by-construction `AppGate` — a plain three-way `when`-switch on `ConsentState` (Unknown → splash, NotConsented → onboarding, Consented → the tab shell) with NO navigation library. Wire the composition root (`App`/`AppDependencies`) over the Phase-18 `DataStoreConsentStore`, add `MainActivity` with edge-to-edge, and prove the signature invariant with a Robolectric+Compose test: health/tab composables are unreachable pre-consent, granting reveals the shell, withdrawing re-gates, and a deep-link flag lands on the gate.

This plan builds the shell and onboarding as minimal-but-real arms (stable `testTag` contracts). Plan 03 enriches the shell (Bloom nav, edge-to-edge insets, predictive back, placeholders); Plan 04 enriches onboarding (hero, disclaimer, privacy, Bloom styling). The gate invariant and its test are LOCKED here and must stay green through both.

Purpose: ASHL-01 core — health UI cannot exist before consent, by construction (the iOS AppGate invariant, ported and test-proven).
Output: A launchable app whose entire routing surface is a consent-gated `when`-switch, with the invariant pinned by a JVM test.
</objective>
