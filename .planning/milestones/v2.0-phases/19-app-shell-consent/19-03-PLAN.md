<objective>
Enrich the minimal `ConsentedShell` from Plan 02 into the real Bloom 3-tab shell: a Material 3 `NavigationBar` wearing the Bloom floating-capsule chrome (surface container, plum selected, inkMuted unselected, petal indicator), edge-to-edge inset handling via `Scaffold`/`WindowInsets` (mandatory at targetSdk 36), predictive-back behavior (Insights/Explore → Log, Log → exit), and Bloom placeholder cards for Log/Insights/Explore that clearly mark their filling phase (Phase 20 for Log, Phase 22 for Insights + Explore).

Purpose: ASHL-03 — the 3-tab shell with edge-to-edge and predictive back handled from day one, so retrofitting insets/back later (the expensive path) is avoided.
Output: A Bloom-styled, inset-correct, predictive-back-aware tab shell rendering honest "arrives soon" placeholders. The Plan-02 tab `testTag` contracts and the consent-by-construction invariant are preserved.
</objective>
