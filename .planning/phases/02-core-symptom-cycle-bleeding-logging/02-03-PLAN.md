<objective>
Build the keystone testable seam of the phase — the pure, value-type `CycleMath` cycle engine and its `CycleSummary` output — entirely as a TDD unit feature with NO SwiftData and NO SwiftUI imports and `today` injected for determinism. It derives a period as a run of consecutive bleeding days, a cycle length as the median of recent start-to-start gaps, and a next-period RANGE with a confidence level (high/medium/low) that degrades gracefully for irregular data.
