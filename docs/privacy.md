# Privacy Policy

_Canonical source: `Sources/EndoApp/Content/PrivacyPolicyText.swift`. Generated by `scripts/sync-privacy.sh` — do not edit by hand._

Your privacy is the whole point.

### Where your data lives
Everything you log in OpenEndo is stored on-device. If you turn on sync, your data is kept only in your own private iCloud account — the same private storage your iPhone backups use. Apple cannot read it, and neither can we.

### No servers, no accounts
OpenEndo has no servers of its own. There is no account to create and no login. Your health data never travels to any backend we operate, because we do not operate one.

### No third-party sharing, no analytics
There is no third-party sharing of your data and no third-party analytics or advertising. We do not embed tracking SDKs. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.

### Who is responsible
This app is built by an independent developer, who is the data controller for the purposes of data-protection law. You can get in touch at sannahrush@googlemail.com.

### Your control
You give explicit consent before any health data is processed, and you can withdraw that consent at any time in Settings. Because your data lives on your device and in your private iCloud, you stay in control of it.
