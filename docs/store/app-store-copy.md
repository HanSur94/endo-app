**Description** (1621/4000)
```
OpenEndo is a free, open-source journal for endometriosis. No account, no subscription, no ads, no analytics. Everything you log stays on your device — and, if you turn on sync, in your own private iCloud.

A CHECK-IN UNDER A MINUTE
• Pain areas on a body map, with severity
• Bleeding, energy, mood, digestion
• Food and drink, sleep, activity, medication
• Free-text notes for the things no list covers
Yesterday's medication, sleep and activity are prefilled. Yesterday's symptoms are not.

WHAT COMES BACK
• Charts you can scrub day by day, with gaps where you did not log rather than invented zeros
• Your cycle phases beside your symptoms, tolerant of irregular or suppressed cycles
• A "possible association" card when two things moved together in your own entries — descriptive, no fitted lines, no score
• A doctor-ready PDF for a date range you choose, with a "self-tracked, not diagnostic" header
• Optional read-only import from Apple Health, so you do not log the same thing twice

PRIVACY IS THE POINT
There is no server of ours, no account to create, no third-party SDK and no advertising identifier. You consent before any health data is processed and can withdraw that consent in Settings. The source code is public, so all of this can be checked rather than believed.

WHAT IT IS NOT
OpenEndo is wellness self-tracking software. It is not a medical device, it does not provide a diagnosis or treatment, and it does not replace care from a clinician. The patterns it shows describe your own entries — bring them to an appointment.

MIT licensed, built by an independent developer. Free now and after 1.0.
```
